MADE FOR THE LPU COMMUNITY

About UniBee

UniBee is a student marketplace for the Lovely Professional University community. It helps students buy and sell useful items and arrange convenient campus pickup.

THE STORY BEHIND UNIBEE

A small campus problem sparked an idea

I’m Abhyudaya Raj, pursuing a BTech in Computer Science at Lovely Professional University. The idea for UniBee came after I lost my charger at the Central Library. I thought I might have to buy a new one and wished there were an easy way to find a second-hand replacement nearby.

That experience inspired a marketplace where students can find and exchange useful things on campus. I’m building UniBee to make campus buying and selling simpler for the LPU community.

You can connect with me on LinkedIn or by email.
