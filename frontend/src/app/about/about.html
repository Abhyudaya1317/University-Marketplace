MADE FOR THE LPU COMMUNITY

About UniBee

UniBee is a student marketplace for the Lovely Professional University community. It helps students buy and sell useful items and arrange convenient campus pickup.

THE STORY BEHIND UNIBEE

A small campus problem sparked an idea

Hi, I’m Abhyudaya Raj, a second-year BTech Computer Science student at Lovely Professional University (LPU) and the founder of UniBee.

The idea behind UniBee came from a simple experience on campus. One day, I lost my charger at the Central Library and realised I might have to buy a new one. That made me wonder: What if there were an easy way to find someone on campus selling a second-hand replacement?

This small incident made me realise that students often have things they no longer need, while others are looking for exactly those items. Yet, there wasn’t a dedicated platform to connect them.

That’s how UniBee was started.

My vision is to build an exclusive marketplace for the LPU community where students can buy and sell products, discover affordable alternatives, and promote their own small businesses.

I want UniBee to be more than just a marketplace. I want it to become a platform that supports student entrepreneurs, encourages reuse, and brings the campus community closer.

UniBee is built by a student, for students — making campus buying and selling simpler, smarter, and more accessible.

You can connect with me on LinkedIn or by email.
