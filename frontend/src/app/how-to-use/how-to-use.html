UNIBEE QUICK GUIDE

How to use

Buy and sell things with people on campus in a few simple steps.

SELLER_GUIDE.EXE

Selling is simple

  1. Choose Sell item in the top menu.
  2. Enter the item name, category, price, photos, and details. Add the campus pickup location where you and the buyer will meet.
  3. Publish your listing. You’ll be notified when a buyer sends a request. Go to Options → Items I’m selling → View items with buyer requests, then accept or decline the request.
  4. Meet the buyer at the agreed campus location and arrange payment directly at pickup.
List an item →
BUYER_GUIDE.EXE

Finding an item is easy

  1. Open Explore and search for an item or choose a category.
  2. Open a listing to review its photos, price, condition, availability, and pickup location.
  3. Select Buy item, enter your quantity and phone number, and send your request. Wait for the seller to accept and confirm a meetup time and place.
  4. Meet the seller at the agreed campus location and pay directly at pickup.
