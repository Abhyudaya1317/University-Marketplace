How to use

Buy and sell things with people on campus in a few simple steps.

SELLER_GUIDE.EXE

Selling is simple

  1. Choose Sell item in the top menu.
  2. Add the item name, category, price, photos, and details.
  3. Post it. You can manage your listing and buyer requests from Options → Items I’m selling.
List an item →
BUYER_GUIDE.EXE

Finding an item is easy

  1. Open Explore and search or choose a category.
  2. Select an item to see its photos, price, and seller details.
  3. Save it, message the seller, or send a request to arrange pickup.
